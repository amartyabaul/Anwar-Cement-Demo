Why Anwar Cement

Every bag, the same
uncompromising standard.

From the clinker we import to the bag we seal, five controlled steps make sure the strength on the label is the strength in your concrete.

01

Premium clinker, hand-picked at source

We import high-grade clinker from selected mines and test every shipment for lime saturation and free lime before a single tonne is unloaded at our Meghna river jetty.

100%shipments lab-cleared on arrival
02

Vertical Roller Mill grinding

European VRM technology grinds finer and more evenly than ball mills, giving a cement that hydrates faster, works smoother and wastes less energy per tonne.

3,400cm²/g Blaine fineness (typical)
03

Precision blending

Fly ash, slag and limestone are dosed by computer-controlled feeders to the exact CEM II/A-M ratio, then homogenised so the first bag and the ten-thousandth are identical.

±0.5%dosing tolerance on every additive
04

Tested every two hours

Our in-house laboratory runs fineness, setting time, soundness and compressive strength on every production lot, around the clock. Nothing ships without a certificate.

6tests on every single batch
05

Sealed, weighed, dispatched

Automatic rotary packers fill each 50 kg bag to weight, moisture-proof liners protect it in transit, and river and road fleets deliver to every district within days.

50.0 kgnet weight, checked on every bag
